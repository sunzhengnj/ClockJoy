Clock-out companion for office workers: playful progress, not an HR tool

A clock-out companion for office workers is a personal, playful way to see how your scheduled day is moving—time left until you planned to leave, optional estimated earnings during those hours, and progress you can glance at without opening another dashboard. In ClockJoy that companion lives on your Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, and Home Screen widgets, using only the schedule and pay you typed in.
