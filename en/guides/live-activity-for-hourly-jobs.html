iPhone Live Activity for hourly jobs: a shift countdown for retail and hospitality

An iPhone Live Activity for hourly jobs is a small card on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island that shows how much of your scheduled shift is left—and, if you want, an estimate of what today’s hours add up to at your hourly rate. In ClockJoy it is built only from the shift and rate you entered, so a cashier, barista, server, or front-desk worker can glance at it on a break without opening anything. It is not a time clock, timesheet, payroll, or attendance record. Estimates ≠ paycheck.

Why a Live Activity fits shift work

  • Breaks are short. The Lock Screen shows the countdown the moment you pick up your phone—no unlocking, no app hunting.
  • Shifts vary. An opening, a close, or a double feel different; a clear end time keeps each one concrete.
  • The last hour drags. Watching minutes drop in the Dynamic Island turns “forever” into a shrinking number.
  • Hourly pay is easy to picture. An optional estimate ticking during scheduled hours makes effort feel visible (see hourly pay widget).
  • Coworkers glance over. Privacy mode hides amounts while the countdown stays (see privacy mode Lock Screen earnings).

Set it up for an hourly shift

  1. Enter your hourly rate. ClockJoy also supports monthly or yearly pay, but hourly is the natural fit here.
  2. Add your shift times. Use the schedule you were given; adjust when a shift changes.
  3. Allow Live Activities. iOS asks for permission; once allowed, the countdown appears on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island on supported iPhones (see clock-out Live Activity).
  4. Pick what to show. Countdown only, or countdown plus estimate. Turn on privacy mode if your phone sits on a shared counter.
  5. Stayed late or left early? Your personal progress can reflect it—motivation only, not overtime pay (see overtime day tracker).

What iOS controls

Live Activities need your permission, and iOS decides how often they refresh and when they appear. Focus modes, Low Power Mode, and older iPhones without a Dynamic Island change what you see. Open ClockJoy for the freshest view. Details: Dynamic Island earnings glance.

Why the estimate is not your paycheck

Hourly pay has many moving parts ClockJoy does not model: tips, unpaid breaks, shift differentials, taxes, deductions, and how your employer rounds punches. The estimate is a motivational number from your own inputs. When you need the real figure, check your pay stub. Estimates ≠ paycheck.

FAQ

What is an iPhone Live Activity for hourly jobs?

A Lock Screen and Dynamic Island card showing time left in your scheduled shift, plus an optional estimate at your hourly rate. In ClockJoy it is personal motivation, not a time clock.

Does ClockJoy clock me in or report my hours?

No. It does not connect to your employer. Keep using your workplace time clock.

Will the estimate match my paycheck?

Not necessarily—tips, breaks, taxes, and employer rules are not included. Your pay stub is the source of truth.
