# Instagram — Live Activity for hourly jobs

## Post

On your feet all shift? One glance on break is enough.

ClockJoy puts your shift countdown on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. Add your hourly rate if you want an optional estimate of what today's hours add up to.

Built for retail, cafe, restaurant, and hotel shifts. Privacy mode hides amounts when coworkers glance over.

Not a time clock. Estimates come from the rate and shift you enter. Not a paycheck, timesheet, tax, or attendance record.
